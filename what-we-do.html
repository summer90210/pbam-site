What We Do

A closer look at how private wealth really works.

Here's a snapshot of what we're focused on this semester — education, guest speakers, and hands-on projects — with more happening throughout the year.

Educational Sessions

Member-led deep dives into the core mechanics of the wealth and asset management sector — from portfolio construction to private banking fundamentals — building a shared foundation across the club.

Guest Speakers

We bring in professionals established in private banking and asset management, alongside recent grads who walked the same path our members are on now, for candid conversation about the industry and how to break in.

Semester Projects

Teams apply what they've learned in sessions to hands-on projects in portfolio allocation and long-term wealth strategy, turning theory into a body of work members can speak to in interviews.

And Beyond That

There's more to PBAM than any one list.

Firm visits and site tours
Networking socials with members and alumni
Resume, interview, and recruiting prep
Cross-club and campus collaborations
