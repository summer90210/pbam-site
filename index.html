Our Mission

SMU's preeminent student-run club for front-office Private Banking and institutional Asset Management.

Our mission at the Private Banking & Asset Management Club (PBAM) is to foster SMU’s premier recruiting pipeline for careers across wealth and asset management by equipping driven students with an understanding of institutional asset management, broker-dealer operations at wirehouses and at independent broker-dealers..

Core Disciplines

Private Wealth Management

Explore how advisors, relationship managers, and investment and lending teams work together to manage complex client needs.

Investments

Evaluate multi-asset investment vehicles across a variety of asset classes and alternative assets (ALTS) in support of portfolio construction.

Macro-Level Economics

Analyze global macroeconomic trends and market cycles to understand their impact on investment decisions.

Client Communication

Develop professional communication through simulated client recommendations, market briefings, and investment discussions.

Student Experience

PBAM was built for SMU students who want to explore hands-on client-financial analysis, investment strategies, tax and estate planning, and client-centric solutions. Additionally, students will explore how private bankers and advisors communicate with investment and lending teams, and navigate the broader financial marketplace through a comprehensive educational curriculum.
